01

What is HONK?

HONK is a deliberately simple Android application built around a single button: the honk button.

Press it, and HONK does what you would reasonably expect a honk button to do.

The project started as a small experiment in making an Android application with essentially no reason to exist.

02

Why?

Not every piece of software needs to solve a serious problem.

Sometimes you just need to press a button and hear:

HONK.

HONK is an experiment in keeping software small, understandable, and fun while still being a real Android application.

03

The important technology

HONK combines a simple interface with audio and vibration feedback to make the button feel appropriately important.

Current state
