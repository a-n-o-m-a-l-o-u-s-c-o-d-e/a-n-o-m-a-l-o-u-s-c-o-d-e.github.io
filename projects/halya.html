01

What is Halya?

Halya is an information and RSS project built around a simple idea:

Stay Informed. Stay Calm.

Instead of treating information as an endless stream competing for attention, Halya explores a quieter way of collecting and presenting it.

02

Why?

There is no shortage of information. The difficult part is keeping up with it without being overwhelmed by everything happening at once.

Halya starts from the idea that staying informed doesn't have to mean constantly checking feeds, notifications, and headlines.

The project is an experiment in making information useful without making it noisy.

03

The idea

Halya puts the focus on information itself rather than the machinery surrounding it.

Current state

Halya is an experimental project and is still evolving.

Its main goal is to explore a better relationship with information: enough to know what's happening, without turning everything into noise.

In other words:

You don't have to read everything.
