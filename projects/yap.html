01

What is YAP?

YAP is an experimental peer-to-peer messaging protocol built in Rust.

Rather than putting a server in the middle of every conversation, YAP is designed around peers communicating directly with one another.

The project is part networking experiment, part protocol experiment, and part excuse to see how far a small Rust codebase can go.

02

Why?

Modern messaging systems usually hide an enormous amount of infrastructure behind a simple message box.

YAP starts from a much simpler question:

What happens if the peers just talk to each other?

That makes YAP useful as a place to experiment with peer discovery, connections, protocol design, binary data, and asynchronous Rust.

03

Architecture

YAP uses QUIC as its transport layer. QUIC provides reliable streams and connections while operating over UDP.
