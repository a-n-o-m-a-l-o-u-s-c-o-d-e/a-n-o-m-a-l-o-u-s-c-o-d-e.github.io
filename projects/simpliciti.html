01

What is Simpliciti?

Simpliciti Player is a lightweight music player built with Python.

The idea is straightforward: give the user a place to play their music without turning the player itself into the main event.

It is a small project built around the principle that a music player should, fundamentally, just play music.

02

Why?

Music software can become surprisingly complicated.

Libraries, recommendations, accounts, subscriptions, social features, and endless interfaces can all get in the way of the basic experience.

Simpliciti starts from a much smaller question:

What if the player was just a player?

03

The idea

Simpliciti keeps the focus on the music rather than everything surrounding it.

Simpliciti Player is an experimental project focused on keeping desktop music playback straightforward.

Play music. That's it.
