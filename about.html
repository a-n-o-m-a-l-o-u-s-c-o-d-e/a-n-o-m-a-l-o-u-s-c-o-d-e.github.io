Why anomalous?

There is no master plan.

Anomalous Code is an independent development project for building things that are interesting enough to exist.

It is a place for software, protocols, experiments, tools, and ideas that don't necessarily fit neatly into one category.

Sometimes something starts because it solves a problem. Sometimes it starts because the question "could I actually make this?" is interesting enough.

Not everything needs to become a product. Not everything needs a business plan. Some things are worth building simply because they are interesting.
